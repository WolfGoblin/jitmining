Commercial terms

Terms across all products

Designed to keep both sides protected from first enquiry to final shipment.

Quantities

Standard minimum order of 500 MT for all commodities. Gold quantities are negotiated per lot.

Delivery

FOB (Free On Board) as standard. CIF and CFR can be arranged, priced to your destination.

Pricing

Indicative prices follow the market and are confirmed in the FCO by volume and Incoterm.

Inspection

Quality and quantity inspection by SGS or Bureau Veritas is mandatory, at the loading point, paid by the seller.

Payment

Telegraphic transfer (TT). The buyer or a verified representative must be on site in Zambia for the final stage.

Documentation

Inspection certificates, origin and export documents and loading records accompany every shipment.
