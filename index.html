Verification

The JIT Verification Standard

We do not ask buyers to pay against promises. Before a lot is offered it is checked, and before it is loaded it is inspected by an independent agency — at our cost.

  1. Company registration and mining or trading licences confirmed
  2. Authority to sell the specific lot confirmed
  3. Physical stock sighted at a named site or warehouse
  4. Weighbridge reading and sampling at warehouse entry
  5. Independent quality and quantity inspection by SGS or Bureau Veritas
  6. Export and origin documents checked before loading
  7. Buyer (or their representative) invited on site to witness

Transaction procedure

How to buy minerals in Zambia: 7 steps

A clear paper trail from first contact to loading, with an on-site visit before any purchase order is signed.

01

Letter of Intent (LOI)

You send a formal LOI with the product, quantity, Incoterm and destination you need.

02

Full Corporate Offer (FCO)

We issue an FCO with the specification, pricing and terms for that lot.

03

FCO acceptance

You review, sign and return the FCO to accept the proposed terms.

04

Draft SPA & proforma

We send a draft Sales & Purchase Agreement and a proforma invoice for approval.

05

Signing & site visit

After mutual signing you (or your representative) visit our site in Zambia for due diligence.

06

ICPO in Zambia

After a satisfactory inspection you sign an Irrevocable Corporate Purchase Order on site.

07

Final SPA & loading

The SPA is confirmed and loading instructions are issued. Inspection happens at the loading point.

FAQ

Questions buyers ask us

Grades, minimum orders, payment, inspection and transport.

How do I buy minerals from Zambia safely through JIT Mining?

Send us a Letter of Intent or RFQ with the product, tonnage and destination. We confirm availability and issue a Full Corporate Offer. After the draft SPA is signed you visit our site in Zambia, then sign an ICPO on site. Every lot is inspected by SGS or Bureau Veritas at the loading point before it ships.

What is the minimum order quantity?

Our standard MOQ is 500 metric tonnes for all commodities. Gold is the exception — quantities are agreed per lot, based on market conditions.

Which Incoterms do you work under?

We mainly sell FOB (Free On Board). CIF and CFR can be arranged, with pricing confirmed for your destination port.

How is payment made?

By telegraphic transfer (TT). The buyer, or a verified representative, must be on site in Zambia for the final stage of the transaction and payment.

Who pays for inspection, and where does it happen?

Inspection by SGS or an equivalent international agency is mandatory. It takes place at the loading point in Zambia and JIT Mining pays for it. Buyers are encouraged to attend or send a representative.

How is cargo moved to the port?

By rail to Dar es Salaam (about 10–14 days) or by road to Durban (about 7–10 days). Ocean freight from the port is arranged by the buyer under FOB, or by us under CIF/CFR.

Can I store cargo before export?

Yes. We provide access to bonded warehouses in Lusaka, Ndola and Kitwe with weighbridge, sampling, loading services and 24/7 security. After a free period, storage is charged per tonne per week.

How are prices set?

Prices follow the market and depend on grade, volume and Incoterm. Copper is referenced to the LME and gold to the spot price plus a premium. Final prices are confirmed in the FCO.
