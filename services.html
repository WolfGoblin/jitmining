Asset valuation

Financial clarity for mineral assets

Mineral asset valuation and financial modelling by qualified professionals, aligned with internationally recognised reporting standards and valuation codes — so the numbers stand up to regulators and investors.

Cost approach

Values the asset on historical and future development costs, grounding the valuation in economic inputs.

Market approach

Benchmarks your project against recent market transactions, reflecting current conditions and investor sentiment.

Cash-flow approach

Discounted cash flow (DCF) models of projected income, showing long-term financial potential.

Investor readiness

Attracting global investment

We help mining companies become investor-ready — especially those looking for investment or joint-venture partners — so they present credibly and raise their chances of securing funding.

  1. Professional pitch decks — data-driven presentations built for institutional and private investors.
  2. Investor matching — introductions between project owners and suitable investors through our network.
  3. Regulatory support — assembling the documents investors need for due diligence, compliant and transparent.
