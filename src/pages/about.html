Who we are

Engineering know-how meets financial discipline

JIT Mining & General Contractors Limited was founded by two Zambian entrepreneurs — an expert engineer and a seasoned corporate accountant. Together they bring deep knowledge of mining, contract facilitation and general supply.

Based in Kitwe, we focus on Zambia, the Democratic Republic of Congo and Tanzania. We supply high-grade mineral commodities to the international market, backed by mining operations experience and our own logistics arrangements.

  • Commodities that meet global standards
  • Professional handling and transparent processes
  • An efficient supply chain from mine to market
