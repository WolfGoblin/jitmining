The JIT Verification Standard

Seven checks before a lot reaches a buyer

Each engagement is measured against these checks, and we tell you plainly which passed, which failed and which could not be completed.

CHECK 01

Legal existence

The company is registered and in good standing with the national registry.

CHECK 02

Licence validity

Mining, trading and export licences are current and match the material.

CHECK 03

Authority to sell

The seller is the owner, or is authorised in writing, for that specific lot.

CHECK 04

Material exists

Stock is sighted, weighed and photographed at a named site or warehouse.

CHECK 05

Independent assay

Sampling and analysis by SGS, Bureau Veritas or an equivalent agency.

CHECK 06

Documents

Origin, export and customs paperwork is checked for consistency and authenticity.

CHECK 07

Ability to deliver

The seller can load the contracted tonnage on schedule.

Transaction procedure

From LOI to loading in seven steps

A clear, documented process — including a compulsory on-site visit — so both sides know where they stand.

01

Letter of Intent

The buyer sends a formal LOI setting out their interest and preliminary requirements.

02

Full Corporate Offer

JIT Mining issues an FCO with commodity specifications, pricing and terms.

03

FCO acceptance

The buyer signs the FCO and returns it as acceptance of the terms.

04

Draft SPA & PI

We issue a draft Sales & Purchase Agreement and a proforma invoice for review.

05

Signing & site visit

After both sign, the buyer visits our operational site in Zambia for due diligence and inspection.

06

ICPO

After a satisfactory inspection, the buyer signs an Irrevocable Corporate Purchase Order in Zambia.

07

Final SPA & loading

The SPA is confirmed and loading instructions are issued.

Quality assurance

Independent inspection on every shipment

To guarantee quality and quantity, every shipment is inspected by an internationally recognised third party.

  • Agency: SGS or an equivalent internationally reputable agency
  • Location: at the loading point in Zambia, before shipment
  • Cost: paid by the seller (JIT Mining)
  • Buyer presence: you or your representative are encouraged to attend

This protocol makes sure every commodity meets the agreed specification before it leaves Zambia.
